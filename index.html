THE BOOTCAMP

The future belongs to people who are ready to build.

An idea can be the beginning of something extraordinary, but turning that idea into a successful business requires knowledge, direction, discipline, and action.

The 2027 Active Leadership & Entrepreneurship Bootcamp is designed to help aspiring entrepreneurs, business owners, young professionals, and emerging leaders move beyond ideas and take practical steps towards building businesses, developing leadership capacity, and creating lasting impact.

Through practical learning, meaningful engagement, and exposure to entrepreneurial principles, participants will gain insights to approach business and leadership with greater clarity and confidence.

Find answers to some questions about the bootcamp.

When is the bootcamp taking place?

The bootcamp holds on 12–13 February 2027.

Where is the venue?

Youth Development Centre, Emos Best Street, Federal Housing Estate, 3-3, Onitsha, Anambra State, Nigeria.

Who should attend?

Aspiring entrepreneurs, business owners, students, graduates, young professionals, and emerging leaders who want to build their skills and move their ideas forward.

How much is registration?

Standard registration is ₦20,000, Residential registration is ₦40,000, and Group registration for five people is ₦75,000.

What does the Residential ticket cover?

Residential registration includes bootcamp registration and lodging.

How does Group registration work?

The group package is ₦75,000 for exactly five participants, which works out to ₦15,000 per person.

How do I register?

Choose your ticket package and complete registration and payment through our Selar registration page.

Can I pay by bank transfer directly to the organisers?

Use the payment options provided on the official registration page. Follow the instructions shown during registration.
